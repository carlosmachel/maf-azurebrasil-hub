# Employee Onboarding

## Onboarding Timeline

| When | What | Owner | Done? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before Day 1 | Background check & signed offer letter | Recruiting | — |
| Before Day 1 | Laptop, badge, and desk assignment | IT + Facilities | — |
| Day 1 — Morning | Welcome session & office tour | People Ops | ☐ |
| Day 1 — Morning | Account setup (email, Slack, GitHub, VPN) | IT | ☐ |
| Day 1 — Afternoon | Meet your onboarding buddy | Buddy | ☐ |
| Week 1 | Complete mandatory trainings (see below) | New hire | ☐ |
| Week 1 | Benefits enrollment (health, dental, 401k) | New hire | ☐ |
| Week 1 | 1:1 with manager — 30/60/90 day goals | Manager | ☐ |
| Week 2 | Access to team repos, boards, and wikis | Team lead | ☐ |
| Week 2 | Shadow a teammate on a real task | Buddy | ☐ |
| Day 30 | 30-day check-in with manager | Manager | ☐ |
| Day 60 | 60-day check-in with People Ops | People Ops | ☐ |
| Day 90 | End of probation review | Manager + HR | ☐ |

## Mandatory Trainings (Week 1)

| Training | Duration | Platform | Deadline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Code of Conduct & Ethics | 45 min | LMS | Day 3 |
| Information Security Basics | 60 min | LMS | Day 5 |
| Anti-Harassment & Inclusion | 30 min | LMS | Day 5 |
| Data Privacy (GDPR/LGPD) | 30 min | LMS | Day 5 |
| Tool Setup Workshop (Git, CI/CD, IDE) | 90 min | Live / Recorded | Day 5 |

## Equipment & Access

New hires receive:
- **Laptop**: MacBook Pro (engineering) or Dell XPS (non-engineering). Request specs upgrade via IT ticket if needed within 7 days.
- **Monitors**: 1 × 27" monitor (request a second via Facilities within 30 days).
- **Peripherals**: keyboard, mouse, headset. Ergonomic assessments available upon request.
- **Accounts**: Google Workspace, Slack, GitHub, Jira, Figma (design), VPN, 1Password.
- **Badge**: building access + gym. Activated on Day 1 at the front desk.

## Buddy Program

Every new hire is assigned an **onboarding buddy** from a different team. The buddy:
- Has a coffee chat on Day 1.
- Is available for "no stupid questions" during the first 90 days.
- Schedules weekly 15-min check-ins for the first month.
- Helps navigate company culture, unwritten norms, and Slack channels.

Buddies are NOT responsible for technical training — that's the team lead's job.

## Benefits Enrollment

Must be completed within **30 days** of start date. If missed, next enrollment window is during annual Open Enrollment (November).

| Benefit | Options | Contoso Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Health Insurance | Standard / Premium / Family | 80% of premium |
| Dental | Basic / Premium | 70% of premium |
| Vision | Single plan | 100% covered |
| 401(k) | Traditional / Roth | Match 50% up to 6% salary |
| Life Insurance | 1× salary (default) | 100% covered |
| Education Fund | Up to $5,000/year | Reimbursement after completion |
| Wellness Stipend | $100/month | Direct deposit |

## Key Policies for New Hires

- **PTO**: 20 days/year (accrued monthly). Available after 30 days. No carryover beyond 5 days.
- **Remote work**: up to 2 days/week after probation (90 days). Full remote requires VP approval.
- **Probation period**: 90 days. Performance reviewed at Day 30, 60, and 90.
- **Expense reports**: see the [expense-report](../expense-report/SKILL.md) skill for reimbursement rules.

1. Use the onboarding checklist: [assets/onboarding-checklist.md](assets/onboarding-checklist.md).

3. All trainings must be marked complete in the LMS — screenshots are not accepted.
4. Benefits enrollment is done via the HR Portal (hr.contoso.com).
