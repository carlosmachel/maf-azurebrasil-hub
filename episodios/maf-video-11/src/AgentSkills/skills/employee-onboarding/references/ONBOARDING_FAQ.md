# Onboarding — Frequently Asked Questions

## First Day & Logistics

**Q: Estou nervoso no primeiro dia. É normal?**
A: Completamente. Seu onboarding buddy existe exatamente para isso — pense nele como seu "Google humano" para perguntas que você não sabe a quem fazer. Ninguém espera que você saiba tudo. Esperam que você pergunte.

**Q: What if my laptop isn't ready on Day 1?**
A: IT prepares equipment 3 business days before your start date. If there's a delay, People Ops will provide a loaner laptop within 2 hours. You won't sit around doing nothing — the Welcome Session and office tour don't require a laptop.

**Q: Can I bring my own keyboard/mouse/monitor?**
A: Yes, but Contoso won't cover repairs or replacements for personal equipment. If ergonomics are a concern, request a free assessment from Facilities — they'll set you up with proper gear.

**Q: I'm remote. Do I still get a buddy?**
A: Absolutely. Remote buddies do virtual coffee chats and are available on Slack. The experience is the same — just with better coffee (yours).

## Accounts & Access

**Q: I can't log into GitHub / Jira / Figma — who do I contact?**
A: File an IT ticket via Slack (#it-help) or email it-support@contoso.com. For Day 1 issues, your buddy can escalate directly. Most access issues are resolved within 1 hour.

**Q: Do I get admin rights on my laptop?**
A: Engineers get admin rights by default. Non-engineering roles get standard access — request elevation via IT ticket with manager approval. Turnaround: 24 hours.

**Q: When do I get access to production systems?**
A: After completing InfoSec training AND receiving team lead approval. Typically Week 2. No exceptions — this is a compliance requirement.

**Q: I need a software license that's not in the standard list (e.g., Postman, Notion). How?**
A: Submit a request in #it-requests on Slack. Licenses under $50/month are auto-approved by your manager. Above that, see the expense-report skill.

## Trainings

**Q: The trainings feel very corporate. Do I really need to do them all?**
A: Yes — they're compliance requirements, not suggestions. The good news: they're short (total ~4 hours), and after that you're free to focus on actual work. Think of it as the "tutorial level" before the real game starts.

**Q: What if I don't finish all trainings by Day 5?**
A: People Ops sends a reminder on Day 4. If you miss the deadline, your manager is notified and you get an extension to Day 10. After Day 10, it escalates to HR. Don't let it get there — it's awkward for everyone.

**Q: Can I skip a training if I did the same one at a previous company?**
A: No. Each company's policies differ, and completion is tracked for audit purposes. The silver lining: if you already know the material, they go fast.

## Benefits

**Q: I missed the 30-day enrollment window. Am I out of luck?**
A: For the most part, yes — you'll need to wait for Open Enrollment in November. The exception is a "qualifying life event" (marriage, new baby, etc.), which opens a 30-day special enrollment window. Contact HR immediately if this applies.

**Q: Can I change my 401(k) contribution at any time?**
A: Yes. Changes take effect on the next pay cycle. You can adjust contribution percentage and investment allocations via the HR Portal anytime.

**Q: What's the wellness stipend? Can I use it for a gym membership?**
A: $100/month, deposited automatically. Use it for gym, fitness classes, meditation apps, ergonomic gear, therapy co-pays — basically anything that keeps you functional and sane. No receipts required.

**Q: Does the education fund cover conference tickets?**
A: Yes — conferences, courses, certifications, and books up to $5,000/year. You must get manager approval before purchase and submit proof of completion/attendance for reimbursement.

## Buddy Program

**Q: My buddy and I don't click. Can I switch?**
A: No hard feelings — tell People Ops and they'll reassign within 48 hours. Chemistry matters, and no one benefits from forced awkwardness.

**Q: Am I supposed to have lunch with my buddy every day?**
A: No. The recommended cadence is: coffee on Day 1, weekly 15-min check-in for the first month, and ad-hoc questions on Slack. Your buddy has their own job too.

**Q: Can my buddy help me with code reviews?**
A: Buddies are for cultural and logistical questions, not technical mentorship. For code reviews, your team lead will assign a technical mentor separately. That said, if your buddy happens to know the answer, nobody's going to stop them.

## Remote & Hybrid Work

**Q: I was told remote work starts after 90 days. Can I negotiate?**
A: The 90-day in-office period is designed for relationship building. Exceptions are rare and require VP approval. After 90 days, the standard is up to 2 days/week remote — discuss your preferred schedule with your manager.

**Q: Do I get a home office stipend?**
A: After the 90-day probation, remote-eligible employees receive a one-time $500 home office setup stipend. Submit receipts via the expense system.

**Q: What about co-working spaces?**
A: Not covered by default. If you're full-remote (VP-approved), you can expense up to $300/month for a co-working membership. Hybrid employees should use the office on their in-office days.

## PTO & Time Off

**Q: I just started. When can I take vacation?**
A: PTO is available after 30 days. You accrue ~1.67 days/month. For the first 90 days, try to limit time off to emergencies — your team is investing in ramping you up.

**Q: What if I get sick during my first week?**
A: Sick days are available from Day 1 — no waiting period. Just notify your manager and take care of yourself. You don't need to "prove" you're sick for absences under 3 consecutive days.

**Q: Can I work from another country for a few weeks?**
A: "Workations" of up to 2 weeks are allowed in approved countries (list on HR Portal), with manager approval and 30 days notice. Tax and compliance implications vary — HR will guide you.

## The Unwritten Stuff

**Q: What Slack channels should I join?**
A: Must-join: #general, #your-team, #it-help, #random. Fun ones: #pets, #food, #music-recs, #today-i-learned. Avoid: #parking-complaints (it's a rabbit hole).

**Q: Is there a dress code?**
A: "Dress for your day." Meeting a client? Business casual. Regular day? Whatever makes you productive. Flip-flops are surprisingly common in engineering.

**Q: Everyone uses acronyms I don't understand. Help?**
A: Check the internal wiki under "Contoso Glossary." If it's not there, ask in #random — someone will answer within minutes, and you'll be doing future new hires a favor when it gets added to the glossary.

**Q: How do I know if I'm doing well?**
A: Your 30/60/90 day check-ins are designed for this. But don't wait — ask your manager for informal feedback anytime. A simple "Am I on track?" goes a long way. No one at Contoso will judge you for asking.
